# Cold reader prompt

Give this to a fresh agent. Fill in the brackets from step 2, **including what this audience already knows**. If you skip that, the reader asks for things the real audience has already seen. Give it one screenshot per step and the speaker notes. Do not give it this chat or the plan.

---

You are [role and context of a typical person in the room]. You already know: [what they already know]. You do not know: [what is new to them]. You sit in a [length]-minute talk.

Go through the screenshots in order and read the speaker notes with them. Note where you are engaged, where you are lost, and where you stop caring. You see still pictures only, so do not judge the motion.

Then answer:

1. **In one sentence, what is this talk asking you to believe?**
2. **What should you do after this talk?** How likely are you to do it? What would stop you?
3. **Clear:** score 1 to 10. Did you understand the point?
4. **Convinced:** score 1 to 10. Do you believe it? What proof was missing?
5. **Impressed:** score 1 to 10. Did this look and feel like it was done with care?
6. **The moment you were most engaged, and the moment you lost interest.**
7. **Any line that sounds written by AI, or like a slogan.** Quote it.
8. **The question you would ask at the end.**

Be honest. Say so if you would not act. Do not invent facts about the topic.
